Bifaria Support

On-device dictation for iPhone

  • Questions or problems? Open an issue and we'll reply there.
  • Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and, if you can, your logs, shared from the Logs tab.

Getting started

Open Settings › Speech and pick a model: Apple (built into iOS) or a Custom Model, which downloads once and then works offline. The first time Bifaria opens after a download, it optimizes the model for your iPhone, which takes a minute or two. Then tap the record button on Dictate, speak, and tap it again. The text is copied and kept in History.

Dictate from any app

In Settings › Shortcut, tap Add to Shortcuts. Then in the iPhone's Settings › Accessibility › Touch › Back Tap, choose Bifaria Dictate for Double Tap. Double-tap the back of your iPhone to start, again to stop, then paste. You can also use the Home Screen widget or the Control Center control.

Free trial and plans

Bifaria is free for 14 days with every feature. After that, recording needs Monthly, Yearly or Lifetime. History, Logs and copying keep working without a plan.

  • Restore a purchase: open Settings › Plan and tap Restore purchase.
  • Cancel a subscription: iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Bifaria.
  • Refunds: are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
